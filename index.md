# Precision Agriculture & GIS — Practical

**University of Sulaimani**  
**College of Agricultural Engineering Sciences**  
**Lecturer:** Rawaz Hama Ali  
**Academic Year:** 2026–2027  
**Language:** English

## Welcome

Welcome to the interactive practical course in Precision Agriculture and Geographic Information Systems (GIS).

This course introduces undergraduate agricultural engineering students to modern digital technologies used in agricultural mapping, field data collection, spatial analysis, remote sensing, and precision crop management.

Students will learn through guided tutorials, hands-on exercises, interactive questions, downloadable datasets, and practical projects.

## Course Learning Objectives

By completing this course, students should be able to:

1. Explain the fundamental principles of precision agriculture.
2. Understand geographic coordinates and coordinate reference systems.
3. Collect and manage spatial information using GPS and GNSS.
4. Create agricultural maps using Google Earth and Google My Maps.
5. Use QGIS for agricultural spatial analysis.
6. Work with vector and raster datasets.
7. Interpret agricultural satellite imagery and NDVI.
8. Create soil, crop, and agricultural management maps.
9. Design and present a practical GIS project.
