# Precision Agriculture & GIS — Practical

**University of Sulaimani**  
**College of Agricultural Engineering Sciences**  
**Lecturer:** Rawaz Hama Ali  
**Academic Year:** 2026–2027

## Welcome

This practical course teaches undergraduate agricultural engineering students agricultural field mapping, GPS/GNSS, QGIS, remote sensing, soil sampling and data-driven management. Each lesson includes a hands-on exercise, an expandable knowledge-check answer, and a submission rubric.

## Learning goals

By the end of the course, students should be able to collect and validate geographic data, create maps in Google Earth and QGIS, work with vector and raster data, interpret crop-related maps cautiously, and submit a reproducible GIS project.

## Software and data

- [Google Earth Pro](https://www.google.com/earth/about/versions/)
- [Google My Maps](https://www.google.com/mymaps)
- [QGIS](https://qgis.org/)
- [Course training datasets](https://github.com/Rawaz83/precision-agriculture-gis-practical/tree/main/data)

All CSV training datasets are synthetic educational examples, **not measured field data or fertilizer prescriptions**.

Practical assignments include a 10-mark rubric. Expandable solutions are for self-assessment, **not automatic grading**. Your lecturer will provide the submission method.
